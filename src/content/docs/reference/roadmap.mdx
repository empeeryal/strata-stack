The template is intentionally opinionated and small enough to understand in an afternoon. These
are the areas under consideration; none of them are promises.

## Planned

Nothing is scheduled at the moment. Everything the roadmap listed so far has shipped: the
[changelog](/changelog) has the details, and the sections below say where to take proposals.

## Non-goals

- A component library beyond what the site itself needs.
- A CMS integration. Content collections accept any loader; wire your CMS through a custom
  loader instead.
- Supporting Node versions older than 24.

## Proposing changes

Open an issue describing the problem first. Pull requests should include a changeset, pass the CI
matrix and update the documentation that describes the affected behaviour.
